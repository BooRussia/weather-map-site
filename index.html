01 / 04
01 · Radar

Forty hours that glide

From 24 hours back to 16 hours ahead, with motion interpolated playback so storms move instead of jump. New frames about every two minutes, plus single site velocity.

02 · Storms

Storms with receipts

Radar storm tracks flagged for hail and rotation, GOES lightning, SPC storm reports and every NWS warning polygon, all tappable.

03 · Hurricanes

The whole hurricane picture

NHC cone, track, warnings, wind field, surge and wind odds, with spaghetti models refreshed every 30 minutes.

04 · Models

Three models, one chart

The European, American and German models side by side for 10 days, with the days they disagree on flagged in yellow, so you know when to be careful.

LiveWorldwide

Pilot cards: winds aloft
and flyability.

Layers → Weather → Aviation → Pilot cards

Winds aloft card

Step an hour at a time through 48 hours. A sounding chart to 18,000 ft shows temperature, dew point, the mixed layer, cloudbase and the freezing line, with winds and temperatures at 3,000 to 24,000 ft. Plus cloudbase, thermals to, lapse rate, freezing level, CAPE, lifted index and CIN.

Flyability card

Pick paraglider, hang glider or drone, and which way your launch faces. The next 12 hours show as green, yellow or red, and tapping an hour lists every reason. Rules of thumb with the reasons shown, never a bare score. You decide.

Drone check

Wind at 400 ft, cloudbase, visibility and the Kp index for GPS, checked against Part 107's 3 statute miles of visibility and 500 ft below clouds.

LiveUS

SIGMETs and G-AIRMETs

Icing, turbulence, IFR, mountain obscuration, wind shear and convective areas, colored by hazard. Tap for severity, base and top, and valid times.

LiveMostly US

Pilot reports

PIREPs colored by the worse of turbulence and icing, with urgent reports ringed in red. Tap for altitude, aircraft and the report as filed.

LiveUS

TFRs and airspace

FAA temporary flight restrictions linked to their NOTAM pages, plus Class B, C and D airspace with every shelf's ceiling and floor.
